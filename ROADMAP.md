# Halfsies Roadmap

These are intentionally **out of scope for the MVP**. They're listed here so we don't forget them.

## Future work

- **In-app card payments.** The MVP uses a "Pay with Venmo" deep link and drivers mark riders as paid by hand. A later version could use Stripe Connect to collect and split payments in the app.
- **Background GPS tracking.** Live location while the app is closed, for safety sharing and arrival estimates. This needs extra OS permissions and battery care.
- **Government ID verification.** Verifying a driver's license and identity (for example with Stripe Identity or Persona), beyond the `@illinois.edu` email check.
- **Other schools.** Support more university email domains and campus-specific pickup spots and quick-pick destinations.
- **Web version.** A browser version of the app. Expo can target web, but maps and some native features would need extra work.
